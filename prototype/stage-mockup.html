KA Engagement Aids · a mock-up, nothing in the app has changed

The engagement's stage, set by you

Home stops being built around the six A's. Each engagement gets one simple drop-down: Awareness, Analysis, Alternatives, Answer, Acceptance or Actions. You set it, and it stays as you left it. The Assessment Roadmap moves under What, beside Understanding a Problem, so the path is Why · What · How.
